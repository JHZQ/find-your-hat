# ⌨ Find Your Hat

Terminal-based video game made entirely in JavaScript and Node.js, player must navigate through the dangerous forest to find their hat.

Be careful of falling down holes or stepping out of the forest!

- Player can move using WASD
- Map is randomly generated with a hat to find and holes to avoid
- Moving through the forest fills previous areas with grass
- Stepping out of the forest or dropping into holes triggers game-over
- Find the hat triggers victory

## 🖥️ Skills used:
- **JavaScript**
- **Node.js**
